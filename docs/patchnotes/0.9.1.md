## Tackle & Fishing

### The fish is on the line

*The fight was read off the rod and the bar; the fish itself was a course, a run timer and a number. Now the number has a body.*

- **From the moment it is hooked, the fish is on the end of your line** — the same drawing the shoal and the tank use, at its true length, tail beating. The line ends on its mouth.
- **It acts the fight out:** a run takes it out along its course — left, right, deep on a sounding; a breach is an arc over the water with a splash on the way out and the way back; a head-shake is a shudder; a tired fish swims slower.
- **Everyone sees it** — it rides the same packet the bar already used, so a friend on the bank watches your fish, not just your rod.
- Client-side: the server sends what the fish is and what it is doing; the client carries where it is, at frame rate.


### Outclassed

*"People have caught 200 lb fish on 6 lb line — it took hours." (idkwho0457_07869) The breaking-strain model already agreed halfway: tension is real and the drag pays out. What it lacked was the other half — a fish that out-pulls the line was simply unwinnable inside its clock.*

- **A fish whose pull is more than the line holds (margin under 0.85) is played out, not reeled.** The fight clock stretches by the margin, up to six times.
- **Winding into a run on a closed drag puts the line straight into overstress** — the bar goes red, and the snap follows unless the drag opens.
- **A crank between runs gains a third** of a normal one.
- **The open drag is how you win:** during a run it no longer bleeds progress — the fish tires against it, and what it loses is what you gain, almost twice as fast with the rod held across the run.
- No message. The bar going red the first time you crank into a run is the lesson.

## Bugfixes

- **The winter rod casts into a drilled hole again.** 0.9.0's cast scan stops at the first block that would stop the tackle falling and lets ice through — but it did not know the mod's own ice hole, so it stopped on the hole and said *no water*. Every ice angler hit it on the first cast (reported, NeoForge 1.21.1).

- **A fish put back where it came out of is never "unfit for this water".** The release judged the column at your feet — the bank — while the fish had been living in the deep water thirty blocks out. The card now remembers where the fish came out, and a release within reach of that spot is judged by that water too. Fish caught before 0.9.1 have no such mark and are judged as before.

## Interface

- **The "Hooked! Reel — but don't over-pull!" line is gone.** The splash, the bent blank and the boss bar say it; a tutorial sentence on every hookup does not. The same for *going deep — don't force it, pump between dives*: a sounding fish is the blank bent down and the drag singing, and nothing else.

## Wiki

- **The breeding calculator draws the fish with its pattern.** The likeliest clutch is painted the way the game paints it — the koi layers under `koiTint`, a carp sprite, and the pattern family's mask under `Pattern.marking` at the mean index — so "banded kohaku" is a picture, not a phrase.
