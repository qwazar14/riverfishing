## Bugfixes

- **The winter rod casts into a drilled hole again.** 0.9.0's cast scan stops at the first block that would stop the tackle falling and lets ice through — but it did not know the mod's own ice hole, so it stopped on the hole and said *no water*. Every ice angler hit it on the first cast (reported, NeoForge 1.21.1).

## Wiki

- **The breeding calculator draws the fish with its pattern.** The likeliest clutch is painted the way the game paints it — the koi layers under `koiTint`, a carp sprite, and the pattern family's mask under `Pattern.marking` at the mean index — so "banded kohaku" is a picture, not a phrase.
