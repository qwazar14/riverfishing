# River Fishing 0.9.0

> **Draft.** New species and more features are still landing in this release; this note grows with them.

---

**Minecraft 1.20.1** (Fabric & Forge) · **1.21.1** (Fabric & NeoForge) · **26.1.2** (Fabric & NeoForge)
· **26.2** (Fabric & NeoForge).

## Bug fixes

**The crash on casting a wooden rod.** Reported by three people, all on NeoForge 1.21.1: cast the stick
rod, the game dies. The line renderer kept a buffer that had already been closed underneath it and drew
the bobber into it. Only float rigs draw a bobber, and the float class is exactly the reel-less rods —
so it read as "the stick rod" when it was any float rod, every cast. Fixed at the root; the rod pod had
the same shape waiting and is fixed with it.

**A cast could land in a lake under the ground.** The scan for water walked straight down from above
your head and took the first water it met, through dirt and stone. The tackle falls, so it now stops at
the first block that would stop it falling. Ice is still fallen through, so a frozen lake still says
*drill a hole*. Known cost: water under a roof has to be cast into from under the roof.

**The rod pod hands over the right rod.** It preferred a bitten rod but could not tell a fish from a
false alarm, so an alarm that had cried wolf on rod one outranked the fish on rod three.

**Reeling no longer swings the rod like an axe.** Every crank played the vanilla arm swing and the cast
whip on top of it. A crank is a crank now; the cast still swings.

## Updates

**Journal: recipes as a bench, catches as fish.** "How to get" was a list of ingredient names; it is
the crafting grid now, slots where the recipe puts them, with tooltips, and a tag ingredient cycles
through what it accepts. A bait's catch list is the fish themselves. The six longest guide pages have
section headings — promoted from the ALL-CAPS leads that were already written, not one word changed,
the same headings in all three languages.

**The fish in the water: who is there, and how they move.** The shoal was chosen by how well the water
suits a species and never by how often it bites, so the water was full of the fish you never catch. It
is bite-weighted now; the rare ones pass through one hour in four; small fish reach the far bank as the
schools they move in instead of being cut for size. And they behave: schools keep their numbers, a fish
beats and glides, a predator that notices your bait comes to look and loses interest, carp roll and
salmon leap. The flat sprite swims with a wave down its body instead of a fold. (Bodies pulled out of
the sprites exist behind `/rfrod fish3d on`, off by default — not there yet.)

## New

**Every fish carries its card.** Hover a fish you caught and it is no longer three grey lines: a
framed card in the colour of its standing — badges for a trophy, a morph, a legend, a prime fish — with
its value at the counter, its size class and sex, where it was caught, what kind of fish it is and
whether it belongs in that water, who caught it, when, and on which rod. Hold Shift for *how*: bait,
water, time, season, weather, the bed it was lying on, the hole or drop-off it was holding on. Two
things stay hidden until a fisherman has looked at it — crouch and show him the fish, one emerald:
its **nature** (timid, wary, greedy, bold) and its **genes**, four loci written down now so that the
fish you catch today is a valid parent when breeding arrives. Fish caught before this release have no
card and keep the old lines.

**Contracts.** The fisherman posts three jobs a day on a board beside his counter. Click one and you
take it as a paper: *three bream from 500 g — from a river, on a float rod, at night*. The fish's own
card says whether it was caught under those terms, so the fish can sit in a chest for a week with the
rod in another one. Bring the paper back with the fish — right-click the fisherman holding it — and he
pays for the set in emeralds, XP and **reputation**. Fish in a keepnet count; the smallest that
qualify are handed over; nothing is taken unless the whole set is there. Two papers at a time, seven
days each, and every fisherman has his own board. Reputation opens a shelf at his counter that nobody
else is shown — at 5, 15 and 30.

**The fish finder is an instrument.**
- **Right-click: the screen.** The water in section along your aim — the real bed metre by metre,
  coloured by what it is made of, the bank where the water stops — with the fish standing where their
  depth is met. Click a species for what the mod knows about it here; click a blocked one for *why it
  will not bite*: wrong depth, wrong season, not this biome.
- **The bottom moves the bite.** A nudge, never a gate: cyprinids like mud and clay, predators rock and
  gravel, salmonids gravel. The species page says how the fish feels about the bed under you.
- **Hold it: the strip.** A sounder trace scrolls on the HUD while the finder is in your hand, so
  walking the bank draws the bed you walked over. Under it, a needle to the nearest hole or drop-off —
  or to the one you picked on the chart.
- **Crouch + right-click: sounding.** A marker cast five blocks wide measures the bed along your aim and
  finds holes and drop-offs. Fish hold on them; casting on one is worth a real bite bonus.
- **The chart.** Everything you have ever sounded, on one face, pan and zoom, kept on your own disk per
  world — the lake in front of you and the one you measured last week. It has holes in it until you
  cast across them.

**The cast gauge reads in metres.** The number over the bar is the distance the line lands at — the
same function the server throws with — on an oak-and-brass gauge with the unreachable band of an
under-loaded rig hatched red.
