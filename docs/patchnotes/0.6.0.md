# River Fishing 0.6.0 — the tackle & fight update

*The mod-page copy of the 0.6.0 notes: paste into CurseForge and Modrinth as is. The full record,
including Russian and Ukrainian, is in [docs/CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).*

---

**Minecraft 1.20.1 · 1.21.1 · 26.1.2 · 26.2** — Fabric, Forge (1.20.1 only) and NeoForge.
**Requires [Architectury API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/architectury-api)**; on Fabric
also **Fabric API**. Serene Seasons and Biomes O' Plenty are optional and the balance assumes them.

**Tackle now has a weight, and weight is a decision.** Before this update a rig was a rig and a lure was a
lure. Now the grams you tie them at decide how far you cast, how long you wait, and which fish will even
look at what you are offering.

## The Tackle Station

Right-click a **Fishing Stall** with an empty hand and it becomes a tackle bench. Pick a form, step the
weight, feed it hooks, iron and string, take the finished tackle out. The block that gives a village its
fisherman is the same block you tie your own gear on.

- **6 bottom rigs and 8 lures**, each with its own weight ladder. Rigs come out with their hooks already
  slotted.
- **Three systems read that weight**: your blank's cast-weight window, the lure-size filter, and cast
  distance. A lure's mass *is* its size — a heavy pilker genuinely silences the small stuff, and it sets a
  floor on how small the hooked fish can be.
- **Dye a lure at the bench.** The colour affects the bite.
- Every piece carries its maker's name and its weight on the tooltip.
- The heaviest classes of spoon, wobbler, jig and castmaster are **sea sizes** and exist only here — the
  sea spinning, boat and trolling blanks finally have tackle inside their test windows.

Hand-crafted lures carry no weight stamp, which the game reads as 0 g: they add nothing to the cast and
never enter the size filter. The recipes still exist and the wiki says plainly that they are the basic
path.

## The fight has physics

- **The rod bends under tension** — six bend steps driven by the real stress on your tackle, and everyone
  nearby sees it, not just you.
- **A running fish loads the tackle by itself.** You no longer have to be cranking for the line to be in
  danger.
- **Fish tire out.** A long fight is winnable by outlasting the fish instead of out-clicking it.
- **Small fry stop fighting like monsters** — a 200 g roach no longer behaves like a carp.
- Opening the drag always pays line, so crouch-spamming is no longer free.
- Trolling starts reliably and the boss bar reads the fight correctly.

## The fisherman is not a fish market

The old trade tables were mostly fish, and gear almost never showed up.

- **Three offers per level** instead of two, drawn from a per-tier pool.
- **Tier 1 always includes one simple fish**, so the first trade is never a wall.
- **Rods are sold fully assembled only** — no more bare blanks you cannot cast.
- Tackle from the stall is **real bench tackle**, carrying the same weight stamp the bench writes.
- The stall also stocks plain vanilla goods — string, a boat, prismarine, a nautilus shell.

## Crafting

- **Every rod blank and every reel is craftable**, on one cost ladder — 24 recipes, no gaps.
- Hooks #2 and #1 joined the hooks tag, which had silently locked the two biggest hooks out of every lure
  recipe.

## Four new river species — 70 in total

**Common dace, Volga zander, White-eye bream** and the **Round goby**, each with its own habitat gates,
fight profile and journal page. Plus a realism pass over the existing species: weights and lengths
corrected against the real fish.

## The wiki, online and in three languages

**[qwazar14.github.io/riverfishing](https://qwazar14.github.io/riverfishing/)** — 18 pages in **English,
Russian and Ukrainian**, one URL with a language switcher. Every rod, reel, line, rig, bait, species and
mechanic with the actual numbers, and all 91 recipes drawn as real crafting grids generated from the
recipe files, so they cannot drift from what the game loads.

## Ukrainian

The mod now speaks **Ukrainian** — all 806 strings, with real Ukrainian angling vocabulary and real
Ukrainian fish names rather than transliterations.

## Fixes

- The rod's assembly screen wrote tackle into a detached copy of the rod, so changes could be silently
  lost. This is the bug behind the "the rod is not assembled" reports.
- The "not assembled" message now names **which part** is missing instead of just refusing to cast.
- Five registry gaps, including the saltwater blanks' durability and the drag curve being computed in two
  places with two different answers.
- Cast-bar and pump-reel corrections from six playtest rounds.
- **On 26.1.2 and 26.2 only:** the four new species rendered as missing-texture squares, and the journal's
  Guides tab ignored clicks. Both fixed.
- The 13 rod icons left over from 0.1.0 are gone.

## Known limitations, stated plainly

- **Hook link, balance and blade size do nothing yet.** They are written to the tackle and shown on its
  tooltip, and nothing else reads them. Groundwork for a later pass — treat them as cosmetic. Weight and
  dye are the two bench choices that actually change how fish behave.
- **The dynamic fish market is missing on 26.1.2 and 26.2.** Prices are static there; on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
  they move with supply.
- **There is no config file yet.** Difficulty is fixed at the *realism* preset in code, so a modpack cannot
  retune it without patching.
