**Minecraft 1.20.1 · 1.21.1 · 26.1.2 · 26.2** — Fabric, Forge (1.20.1 only) and NeoForge.
**Requires [Architectury API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/architectury-api)**; on Fabric
also **Fabric API**.

A fix release. Every item here came from a player report on the Discord and on minecraft-inside — three
of them from the same week 0.6.0 shipped.

## The cast no longer disappears on its own

**This is the important one.** The mod checks that you are still holding the rod your cast was made
with, and it was making that check by comparing the rod's item **by memory reference**. That reference
goes stale the moment anything rewrites your inventory slot — which happens far more often on a server
than in single player. From that moment the mod believed you had swapped rods, and it ended the cast
**silently, with no message at all**.

From the bank that looks exactly like the rod reeling itself back in a moment after you cast. It hit
every rod class, which is why it was reported for lures and for bait alike, and it was much worse in
multiplayer. It now tracks the hotbar slot instead, which cannot go stale.

## The Fishing Stall opens its bench on Forge

On **1.20.1 Forge only**, right-clicking a Fishing Stall with an empty hand opened the Tackle Station
server-side but never drew the screen — the block simply did nothing. The screen was registered on every
other loader. Fixed.

## The mod no longer tells you to fish the wrong way

- The message after a lure cast said **"Hold right-click to retrieve"**. Holding is the one input that
  cannot work: it auto-repeats about five times a second, which winds the lure in at full speed *and* is
  too fast a cadence for a fish to take, by the mod's own design. The guide page said the opposite. The
  cast message now says what actually works — individual clicks with a rhythm.
- **Every rod's tooltip now says how that rod is fished**, on the first line: *active* rods are worked
  with rhythmic clicks, *float* and *bottom* rods are cast and then left alone. Before this, a bottom rod
  and a spinning rod carried word-for-word the same hint, and nothing in the game distinguished them.
  (The winter rod is exempt — it is jigged through an ice hole, and already says so.)
- **A new journal guide covers the waiting flow**, the float and bottom rods. The journal had thirteen
  guide pages and not one of them was about the two rod classes you do not retrieve, while three of them
  taught you to crank.

## Wiki

The bottom-rod section now states plainly that these rods are not retrieved and that a right-click
outside a bite reels the cast in. In all three languages.
