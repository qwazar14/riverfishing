# Contracts, reputation and nets

New in **0.9.0**. The fisherman's board is the first thing in the mod that *takes* fish rather than selling you tackle, and the reputation it pays in is what a net spends.

## The board

Every fisherman posts **three jobs a day** on a board beside his counter. Click one and you take it as a **paper**: *three bream from 500 g — from a river, on a float rod, at night*. A post you took is greyed for everyone, and every post says when the board turns over.

- **Two papers at a time**, **seven days** each.
- One post in two on the **third line** is a **fry order** — twenty, thirty or forty fry of a species, paid like a contract. See [Breeding](breeding.md#fry).

## The terms

The fish's own [card](species.md) says whether it was caught on the paper's terms — species, weight from, water body, rod class, time of day, season, bait — so a fish can sit in a chest for a week with the rod in another one. Fish in a **keepnet** count. Nothing is taken unless the whole set is there; the smallest fish that qualify are the ones handed over.

Hand in by **right-clicking the fisherman while holding the paper** with the fish on you.

## Pay

Emeralds, journal XP and **reputation**. Reputation is a signed number on the board; at **5, 15 and 30** points the counter opens a shelf nobody else is shown. A clutch of **roe** sells at the counter too — sturgeon and salmon roe at three times the fish's price.

## Nets, and what they cost

A rod takes the one fish that was hungry. A net takes everything, including next spring. Two nets, both without a rod:

| Net | Rate | Life |
|---|---|---|
| Seine net | 1–3 fish a minute | 64 hauls |
| Cast net | at most one fish every 15 s | 32 hauls |

A netted fish carries a card that says **NETTED** — and **POACHED** when the water was not yours. It never loses that, and a poached fish put back into the water buys no pardon.

Nets are for the pond you stocked yourself: in a [claimed pond](blocks.md#private-pond-sign) your own net is legal and the wait between hauls is a third. Haul from anyone else's water, or from wild water, and every fisherman within earshot saw it:

- **Reputation −5 per haul.**
- The water empties **twice** as fast as the catch itself accounts for.
- Under zero the trusted shelf shuts; at **−15** the board is blank.
- **Restitution** is mature fish released into *wild* water — **5 kg per point**.

A net still pays the water's ledger the way a rod does: a settled species from its head count, an unsettled brood from its book — see [Stocking](stocking.md).
