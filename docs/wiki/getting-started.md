# Getting started

From an empty inventory to your first fish in about five minutes. Vanilla fishing rods do nothing here — everything below is River Fishing gear.

## 1. Dig up some bait

Take a **shovel** to dirt, a grass block, coarse dirt, podzol, rooted dirt, farmland, a dirt path or mud. Each block you break has a **10 % chance** to pop a **Worm**.

Worm is the single most widely liked bait in the game — 38 of the 79 species rate it, 21 of them at 0.9 or better. Six or seven will get you started.

Other easy sources: **Maggots** from zombies (33 %), **Bloodworms** from drowned (33 %), **Chicken Liver** from chickens (25 %).

## 2. Craft the starter kit

Three items. None of them costs more than one iron nugget.

**Stick Rod** — three sticks on the diagonal:

```
· · S
· S ·
S · ·
```

**A hook.** One Iron Nugget gives you **two Hook No.16**. Add a nugget to a No.16 to get a No.14, again for a No.12, and so on. Bigger number = smaller hook, so No.16 and No.14 are the small-fish hooks you want now.

**A line.** A **ring of 8 String** (empty centre) makes **two Mono Line 0.10**. That works, but most starter fish want **0.14** — put a 0.10 line in the centre of another string ring to thicken it one step.

```
S S S        S S S
S · S   →    S L S     (L = the previous line)
S S S        S S S
```

Nice to have: a **Fishing Journal** (Book + Hook No.12 + Leather) — it holds a bestiary, a how-to page per species, your quests and the written guides.

## 3. Assemble the rod

Hold the Stick Rod and **sneak + right-click**.

The Stick Rod is reel-less, so there is no reel slot — drop the **line** straight in. Its built-in **Primitive Rig** is shown inline with two slots: a **Hook** and a **Bait**. Put your No.14 hook in one and a Worm in the other.

The rod's tooltip turns green: **Rod assembled**.

> On rods that *do* take a reel, the **reel goes in first** — the line spools onto the reel. The screen tells you if you get it wrong.

## 4. Find water

Any pond or river will do. Small water is species-poor, so a **river or a lake** gives you more to catch, but a pond is fine.

Sixteen species live in **every** water in the world, so nowhere is ever dead: Bream, Crucian Carp, Roach, Rudd, White Bream, Perch, Gudgeon, Ruffe, Bleak, Bluegill, Mackerel, Herring, Rotan, Nase, Smelt and Round goby.

Bear in mind the **Stick Rod cannot cast past 6 blocks** — it is a fixed length of line on a tip. Fish the margins.

## 5. Cast

**Hold** right-click to charge, **release** to throw. There is no click-cast.

The power bar appears at the bottom of your screen. It is a triangle wave: power climbs to full over **one second**, then falls back over the next second, and repeats. **Release at the peak for the longest throw** — overcharging weakens it. Tick marks sit at 50 % and 85 %.

| Bar colour | Power |
|---|---|
| Green | under 50 % |
| Yellow | 50–85 % |
| Red | above 85 % |

If the bar has a **dim red band with a hard cut-off** at its far end, your rig is too light for the blank and it physically cannot throw that far. See [the test window](rods.md#loading-the-blank-the-test-window).

The action bar confirms: *"Cast out. Wait for a bite…"*

## 6. Watch the float

**There is no "Bite!" message and no sound.** The bobber plunges on your screen and that is the entire cue — spotting it is the game. A float rod gives you **3.6 seconds** from the plunge to react.

If nothing is going to bite here at all, the cast tells you up front: *"Nothing is biting here right now"*. And if the water is dour, *"The fish are wary of this setup — it could be a very long wait"* — change bait, move, or feed the spot.

## 7. Strike

Right-click when the float goes down.

On a **reel-less** rod (Stick, Bamboo, Pole) this is a plain click — no timing bar. The one timing challenge comes next.

## 8. The pull-out

Immediately after the strike a **timing bar** appears low on your screen with a moving yellow marker:

- **Green zone** — a certain landing.
- **Orange flanks** — a 25 % chance.
- Outside both — the fish throws the hook.

Click when the marker is in the green. That is your fish. The bigger the fish, the faster the sweep and the narrower the zone, and a bare Stick Rod is the least forgiving blank there is — which is exactly why a real Pole Rod is worth building.

Landed: *"Caught: Roach — 180 g, 18 cm"*. The item carries that species, weight and length forever, and no two catches stack.

## Reading the HUD

| Element | Where | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| **Cast power bar** | Bottom centre, while charging | Throw distance; the red band is unreachable |
| **Timing bar** | Bottom centre, on a bite | *"Strike in the green zone!"* — green certain, orange 25 % |
| **Boss bar** | Top of screen, during a fight | Fill = landing progress. **Green** tension fine, **Yellow** two-thirds to the limit, **Red** at the limit or the fish is running. The title reads *"🎣 You"*, *"— running!"* or *"— tiring"* |
| **Fight coach** | Just under the boss bar | `⇪ reel` · `⇩ ease off!` · `⚠ open the drag!` |
| **Action bar** | Above the hotbar | Everything else: cast confirmations, warnings, catches |
| **The rod in your hand** | — | Visibly **bends** with the tension on the line |
| **The line's colour** | — | Warm white = mono, dark moss green = braid, pale ice blue = fluorocarbon |

## Your first fight (on a reeled rod)

The Stick Rod skips this, but the moment you build anything with a reel, the fight becomes the game. Three positions, no new keys:

| Position | How | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Crank** | Right-click | You gain line. Tension climbs. |
| **Rest** | Stand still, don't click | Tension bleeds off. |
| **Open the drag** | **Crouch** | Tension drops 3× faster and **the line cannot snap** — but cranks gain nothing and a running fish takes line. |

The rules that matter:

1. **When it runs, stop cranking.** Cranking into a run spikes tension and gains almost nothing.
2. **When it jumps, crouch.** Reeling through a jump rips the hook out 35 % of the time.
3. **You only gain standing up.** Crouching forever is safe but goes nowhere.
4. At 85 % there is one **guaranteed last dash at the bank**. Ease off through it.

Over-tension does not snap instantly — every tick past the limit rolls a chance that grows the longer you hold it. Brief spikes are survivable; cranking through a whole run is not. When it does break, the message names the load in kilograms, which tells you exactly what tackle you needed.

Full detail: [the fight](fishing-mechanics.md#the-fight).

## What to do next

| Goal | Do this |
|---|---|
| Stop digging for bait | Build a **[Worm Farm](blocks.md#worm-farm)** (8 planks + dirt) and a **[Maggot Farm](blocks.md#maggot-farm)** (8 planks + rotten flesh) |
| Catch more, faster | Upgrade to a **Bamboo Rod** (a float rig) then a **Pole Rod** (two hooks, two baits, the best pull-out curve) |
| Pull fish to you | Throw **[groundbait](groundbait.md)** into the water — Wheat Seeds + Bread makes Base Groundbait; add up to 8 other things to it for more |
| Cast further, catch bigger | Build your first **reel** and a **Feeder Rod** — that opens the Bottom flow and the [rod pod](blocks.md#rod-pods) |
| Catch predators | A **Spinning Rod** and a lure. Pike and zander **bite through a bare line** — fit a leader |
| Know what's in the water | The **[Fish Finder](tools.md#fish-finder)** lists what can bite here right now |
| Find direction | The journal's **Quests** tab. Stage 1 is five easy goals; *Discover 8 species* hands you a Spinning Rod |
| Sell your catch | Place a **[Fishing Stall](villager.md#the-fishing-stall)** to make a Fisherman. He only buys **prime** fish — specimens at 70 % or more of their species' maximum weight |

## Common early messages

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *The rod is not assembled* / *No line on the rod* / *No rig on the rod* | Sneak + right-click and fill the missing slot |
| *No reel — fit the reel first, the line spools onto it* | Reel before line on a reeled blank |
| *No bait on the hook* | The cast will go, but nothing will take |
| *A pole cannot reach past 6 blocks* | Reel-less rods have a fixed reach |
| *No water to cast into* | Aim lower, or at actual water |
| *Water too narrow for a long cast* | A long-range blank on water under 12 blocks wide — every fish's odds drop to 40 % |
| *The rig is too light for this blank — short throw* | Rig weight below the blank's test window |
| *Rig too heavy for this blank — risk of breakage* | Above the window — your line will snap much sooner |
| *This spot is fished out — move on* | You have depleted this chunk; it recovers over ~25 minutes |
| *Mistimed! You missed the strike window* | The marker was outside the zone |
| *Snagged solid — lost the rig* | A dead snag (3 % per cast) |
| *Empty strike — the hook was blunt* | Sharpen it with a [Whetstone](tools.md#whetstone) |
